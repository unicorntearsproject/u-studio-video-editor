# Getting started

[Docs home](../README.md) › [User guide](README.md) › Getting started

This walk-through cuts a recording into a short clip and exports it. It
takes about five minutes.

## The window

The window opens maximised; un-maximise it for a smaller one.

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Header bar** (top) | New, Open, Recent, Save, Import, Render, Undo/Redo, Settings (tools) and Help (`?`). With the Titles add-on, a **T** between Render and Settings opens U-Stu Titles, the title designer. The title shows the project's size and frame rate. |
| **Media browser** (left, collapsible) | Every file you've imported, with thumbnail, length and badges. |
| **Preview** (centre) | The picture at the playhead. You can also place and resize pictures here. |
| **Transport bar** (under the preview) | Play, step, shuttle, split (scissors), Ripple, volume, preview scale. |
| **Timeline** (bottom) | Tracks and clips, with a timecode ruler, a cyan playhead and markers. |

Hover over any button to see what it does and its shortcut. **Help ›
Controls** describes every button and gesture.

## Cut a clip in five steps

1. **Import.** Press `Ctrl+I` and pick your recording, or drag it from the
   file manager onto the timeline. A new project takes its size and frame
   rate from the first video you import.
2. **Find the moment.** Press `Space` to play, and `J`/`K`/`L` to shuttle
   backwards, stop and forwards (press `L` again for 2×, 4×, 8×). The arrow
   keys step one frame at a time. Waveforms on each clip show where people
   are talking.
3. **Cut.** Put the playhead where the good part starts and press `X` to
   split. Do the same where it ends.
4. **Clean up.** Click an unwanted piece and press `Shift+Delete` to remove
   it and close the gap. Plain `Delete` leaves a gap instead.
5. **Export.** Click **Render…**, choose where to save, and the MP4 renders
   in the background while you keep working. When it's done, the button
   turns cyan: click it to open the file.

Your work is autosaved two minutes after each edit. Press `Ctrl+S` to save
the project under a name of your choice.

## Recipes

- **Podcast with separate mic and camera recordings:** put each recording
  on its own track, select both, right-click the one that should stay put,
  and choose **Sync Tracks (Audio)**. See [Audio](audio.md).
- **Webcam in the corner:** put the webcam clip on the track above the main
  video, click its picture in the preview, and drag a corner handle to
  shrink it. See [Preview and transform](preview-and-transform.md).
- **Logo watermark:** drop a PNG onto an empty top track. A still image
  stretches to the end of the project automatically.
- **Several cuts for different platforms:** render profiles set the output
  height and quality. Right-click Render to queue one render per profile.
  See [Rendering](rendering.md).
